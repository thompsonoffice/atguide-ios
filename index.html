ATGuide Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the ATGuide iPhone app, which is listed on the App Store as ATSpotlight.

Effective November 15, 2026

App: ATGuide (iPhone). Developer: Hunter Thompson.

Summary

ATGuide does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The developer, Hunter Thompson, receives no data from the app.

Works offline

All reference data is included in the app. ATGuide makes no network requests of its own and works with no internet connection.

No accounts, ads or tracking

There are no accounts, sign-ins, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party software development kits.

What stays on your device

Your favorites, recently viewed entries, comparison picks and text size setting are saved only on your own device, in the app's local settings. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. They may be included in your own device backups, which Apple handles under its privacy policy.

Links and sharing

When you choose a source link, it opens in your web browser and that website's own privacy policy applies. When you choose Share as TXT, the text goes only where you send it.

Children

ATGuide collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear in the app and on its web page with a new effective date.

App Store privacy label

Data Not Collected.
